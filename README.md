# Cly

**Cly is the system of record for computational research.**

It connects questions, sources, hypotheses, experiments, artifacts, decisions, and claims into one local-first, auditable research workspace.

Cly is not an IDE. Researchers keep working in VS Code-compatible editors, Jupyter, terminals, and GitHub; Cly brings research context, provenance, and approvals into those tools through companion integrations.

## What Cly answers

- What are we trying to discover?
- Which sources, runs, and artifacts support or contradict a claim?
- Which external code, notebook, commit, environment, and dataset produced a result?
- What should happen next, and what requires human approval?

## Delivery focus

1. Research core and local Cly service
2. Research graph, evidence, sources, and planning
3. Experiments, provenance, decisions, and reproducibility
4. Companion integrations for existing research tools
